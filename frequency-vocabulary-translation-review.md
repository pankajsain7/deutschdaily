# Frequency-vocabulary translation review

Reviewed scope: 2,525 frequency cards in `src/frequency-dictionary.json`.

Each card was assessed for (1) the German headword and English gloss, (2) the
German example sentence, and (3) the naturalness and faithfulness of its English
translation. The source is generally strong: most entries are accurate and the
English reads naturally. The items below need correction or a deliberate editorial
decision.

## Data-quality observations

- The dictionary combines formal, colloquial, technical, legal, and adult/crime-related source sentences. The translations are usually faithful, but the register is inconsistent for a beginner-focused learning product.
- Several headwords use parenthetical forms such as `(he)raus`, `d(a)rin`, and `vorn(e)`. These are useful search aliases but are not standard display forms; consider showing a standard form first and aliases separately.
- A few glosses list senses not represented by the example sentence. This is acceptable in a dictionary, but a learner-facing card is clearer when the displayed gloss prioritizes the sense used in its example.
