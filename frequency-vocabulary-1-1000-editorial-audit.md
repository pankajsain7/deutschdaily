## Editorial standards used

- Headword glosses describe the form and sense actually shown on the card; they
  do not mix unrelated parts of speech merely because their spelling is similar.
- English keeps the German person, number, tense, modality, and degree of
  certainty unless English requires a natural equivalent.
- Colloquial shortened forms are labelled as colloquial and shown separately
  from semantically different words, such as *rüber*, *drüber*, and *drauf*.
- German infinitive clauses have the required comma, and reflexive verbs retain
  their reflexive pronoun.

## Validation

The corrected data must pass the project validation and translation-audit
scripts, and the two dictionary representations must remain identical.
